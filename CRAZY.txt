*********** CRAZY ******************

1. Coinfinder Function 
    - Scan all listed coins on Binance and filter them based on price which is above the Hull Moving Average on both the 4-hour and 1-day timeframes

2. Entry Conditions
    - A long position is opened when the price crosses above the 1-hour HMA
    - A short position is opened when it crosses below
    - Positions are limited to a Max_positions

3. Position Management
    - Positions are added based on volume scale for every 2% gain, with incremental amounts set to a total of $162.5
    - Higher leverge means higer risk
    - If the price moves 2% in the opposite direction, the position is closed
